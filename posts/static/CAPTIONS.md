# Static posts: Hop

Six page images (2880×1800, `posts/static/`) in two X posts of three images, one caption each.
Written to the X caption brief (Hop named once, no hashtags, emoji or hire-me line), with the
user's changes: each caption opens with the owner's point of view, then one line per screen in
image order, so every screen in the post is covered. Each has a one-line group-chat version.
The desktop showcase and foldable-iPhone images don't get captions.

```text
Running a small fashion brand means checking every number yourself. Hop checks them first.
Analytics: ask what needs attention and get a ranked list.
Sales: click a day on the chart and the whole page follows.
History: every question is saved with a snapshot of the page.
```

Group chat: Part one of Hop: the AI ranks what needs doing, the sales chart works as a filter,
and History keeps what everyone was looking at.
